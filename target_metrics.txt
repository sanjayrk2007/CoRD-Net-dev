4. External-validation target
For a clinically stronger claim, aim for:

Metric	External target
QWK	≥0.82
Balanced accuracy	≥0.68–0.70
Macro-F1	≥0.65
KL1 recall	≥0.35–0.40
Extreme-grade errors	Very low or absent
An external study reported QWK 0.820, balanced accuracy 0.693, and KL1 recall 0.372 
Vaa25
. Another recent framework showed internal accuracy/F1 of 0.726/0.726 falling to 0.685/0.656 externally, illustrating why external testing matters.

5. Requirements beyond numerical scores
For a defensible SOTA claim, also require:

At least three random seeds, preferably five.
Mean, standard deviation, and 95% confidence intervals.
A locked test set that was not used for model selection.
Patient-level overlap audit and duplicate-image audit.
Comparison with ConvNeXt/STN baselines under the same split.
Per-grade precision, recall, F1, and confusion matrices.
KL1/KL2 boundary-error analysis.
External or temporal validation.
Calibration analysis if claiming useful uncertainty estimation.
Also verify the loader: the shown __getitem__ returns only [global_img]; it does not visibly load medial or lateral crops. Therefore, confirm that the stated compartment-aware architecture is actually receiving those inputs.

Final target
For your project, I would set the main goal as:

Patient-wise QWK ≥0.89, accuracy ≥72%, macro-F1 ≥0.74, MAE ≤0.32, KL1 recall ≥0.50, and KL1 F1 ≥0.45, reproduced across at least three seeds.

Your current FGBF result is a useful starting point, but it is not yet SOTA.
